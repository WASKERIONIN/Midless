The .vox models in models/vox/ are the official sample models by
ephtracy, from the MagicaVoxel / voxel-model project
(https://github.com/ephtracy/voxel-model).

MagicaVoxel license: "Free to use for any project. Credits to the
software are appreciated." - https://ephtracy.github.io

They are rendered in-game as decorative sub-block landmarks on the
starter island (visual only, no collision).
